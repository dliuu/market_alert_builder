Session Brief
design proposal

Two emails per trading day.
One prepares you, one accounts for you.

09:30 open
16:00 close
08:15 · brief one
16:45 · brief two

Both sends sit outside the bell — you read them when you can act on them, not while the tape is moving.

One screen, and you should never need to visit it twice. A sector is a folder, a ticker is a line, and every line is either owned or watched. Share count is optional — but supplying it is what upgrades the close email from percentages to dollars.

Each row carries its lifetime unrealised P&L, and each sector header carries today’s direction — so the setup screen doubles as a standing position sheet.
